# E2E-076-a-byte-finding-names-what-an-exception-would-accept: a byte detail carries the ceiling too

A byte or token detail is `<path>: <actual> <unit>` with no budget clause, so it
had no parenthesis to extend (§FS-004-check-audit.1). It opens one of its own:
the ceiling a plain `fissile exception add` would record is as much use to a
caller looking at a blob as to one looking at a source file, and each unit takes
its own `[exceptions.bump]` step (§FS-001-config.5, §DF-006-quantized-ceilings.1).

250 bytes against a 100-byte step is 300.
